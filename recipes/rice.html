Rice

Description

Mexican Fried Rice is a vibrant, fusion dish that adapts the high-heat,
fast-cooking framework of Asian stir-fry using traditional Mexican-American ingredients and flavors.

Ingredients

  • Base Rice: 2 to 3 cups
  • 1 diced white or yellow onion
  • 1 diced jalapeño or poblano pepper
  • Diced bell peppers
  • 1/2 to 1 cup of corn kernels
  • 1/2 to 1 cup of black beans or kidney beans

Steps

  1. Prep the Rice
  2. Cook the Protein
  3. Sauté the Aromatics and Vegetables
  4. Season the Base
  5. Fry the Rice
  6. Garnish and Serve
